Working Papers

Plastic Waste Trade and Environmental Leakage
Solo-authored

Examines cross-border plastic waste flows, market failures, and environmental externalities in developing countries. Combines international trade data with health and firm-level data to investigate the local impacts of plastic waste imports on Chinese communities and industries.

Relational Contracts and Agricultural Supply Chains in Kenya
with Russell Morton (University of Rochester) and Eleanor Wiseman (University of Wisconsin–Madison)

Uses original fieldwork and survey data to study relational contracts and contract structure in the form of trade credit in agricultural markets.
