Working Papers

Plastic Waste Trade and Environmental Leakage
Solo-authored
  • Combines Chinese customs data with health, environmental, and firm-level datasets to examine how import restrictions on plastic waste reshape private market behavior across upstream and downstream sectors.
  • Studies how regulatory shocks affect importing firms, market structure, and local environmental and health outcomes.
Relational Contracts and Agricultural Supply Chains in Kenya
with Russell Morton and Eleanor Wiseman
  • Examines trade credit, enforcement mechanisms, and relational contracts between retailers and traders in agricultural value chains.
  • Uses original field data to analyze how informal financing arrangements influence market participation and behavior.
